AVOID: purple/neon gradients, generic SaaS hero layouts, stock-template look, emoji as icons, "crypto casino" visuals, cannabis cliches (rasta colors, smoke-shop vibes), heavy drop shadows, walls of text, motion that is chaotic, jittery, or blocks reading.

DEVICE-AWARE ENHANCEMENT RULES
- Gate effects with matchMedia: (hover: hover) and (pointer: fine) for cursor, tilt, spotlight, magnetic effects. (pointer: coarse) gets touch-friendly alternatives.
- Reduce effects automatically if navigator.hardwareConcurrency <= 4 or deviceMemory <= 4 or the connection is slow (saveData).
- Handle resize and orientation change cleanly: refresh ScrollTrigger, recalc pinned sections, no layout jumps.

MOTION AND EFFECTS SYSTEM
Libraries: GSAP + ScrollTrigger (scroll choreography, pinning, scrubbing; manual text splitting if SplitText is unavailable), Framer Motion (component and layout animation, presence), Lenis (smooth scroll synced with ScrollTrigger; use native momentum scroll on touch devices instead of hijacking it). Optional lazy-loaded canvas/WebGL layer (OGL or Three.js) on desktop only.
Global easing: cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1) for UI, expo-out for reveals. Durations: 300-700ms UI, 900-1400ms hero and section reveals.

A. Preloader (max 1.8s desktop / 1.2s mobile, skippable, first visit per session only)
Mascot silhouette fills with color while a counter runs 0 to 100, then the screen splits and wipes away (clip-path reveal) into the hero. Content must remain crawlable.

B. Custom cursor (desktop fine-pointer only)
Small gold dot plus a lagging soft ring (spring physics). Ring grows over links/cards with a label ("View", "Join"), becomes a magnetic blob on buttons, shrinks on press. Never hide the native cursor on text inputs.

C. Hero choreography
- Animated gradient mesh drifting slowly.
- Floating soft leaf and pollen particles drifting upward (canvas), pausing when the tab is hidden or the hero is off-screen; subtly repelled by the cursor on desktop.
- H1 reveals line by line with masked slide-up and staggered word/character animation. A gold hand-drawn underline path draws itself (SVG stroke-dashoffset) under "Natural Relief", "Patient Advocacy", and "Web3 Wellness" in sequence.
- Subheadline fades in with a blur-to-sharp transition.
- Buttons enter with a spring pop, shine sweep, sliding arrow, gold glow pulse; magnetic hover on desktop, press-scale on touch.
- Mascot enters with scale-and-fade plus a small bounce, then loops a slow float (6-8s), shadow squash-and-stretch, and a pulsing green radial glow. Mouse parallax on desktop; gentle auto-drift on mobile.
- Rotating circular text badge "MEDICAL CANNABIS & WELLNESS · " orbits the mascot, speed increasing slightly with scroll velocity.
- Floating glass chips ("Education First", "Patient Led", "Community Driven") bob on independent sine paths (on mobile, keep them inside the viewport, smaller, and non-overlapping with text).
- Soft smoke/vapor wisps curl above the mascot, echoing the logo.
- Scroll cue: animated line draws down and loops (desktop); hidden on mobile.
- On scroll out of the hero, the mascot scales down and drifts, text parallaxes slower.

D. Scroll-driven storytelling
- Thin gold scroll-progress bar at the top.
- Section transitions use overlapping clip-path or curved SVG dividers that morph on scroll.
- Large outlined display words ("RELIEF", "ADVOCACY", "WELLNESS") as a velocity-reactive marquee between sections (slight skew with scroll speed), clipped in an overflow-hidden wrapper.
- Background color shifts smoothly from sky/mint to cream to deep teal across sections.
- Pillars: on desktop the header pins briefly while cards stagger in from alternating directions with slight rotation settling to zero; on mobile use simple staggered fade-up with no pinning.
- Launch: on desktop a short pinned scrub fills the timeline, lights nodes in sequence, and rolls in the countdown tiles; on mobile the same sequence plays on scroll without pinning.

E. Card and component effects
- Pillar cards: desktop 3D tilt (4-6 degrees), cursor spotlight, glare, border that traces a gradient light, accent glow in each card's own color. Icon animations: flask bubbles rise, bone joint flexes, shield pulses with the cross glowing. On touch, trigger these icon animations on tap and when the card enters the viewport center.
- Numbered labels (01/02/03) count up or flip in.
- Text effects: headings reveal word by word; eyebrow labels use a short text-scramble on enter; key phrases get animated gold highlight swipes.
- Buttons and nav links: animated underline, ripple on click/tap.
- Countdown: digits roll vertically (odometer style), tiles have a subtle breathing glow, a thin ring progress animates around the seconds tile.
- Timeline nodes pulse softly; the active node has a radar-ping ring.
- Final CTA: mascot peeks from the edge, waves or bobs, and follows the cursor on desktop (auto-bob on mobile); a small tasteful canvas leaf burst fires on first CTA hover (desktop) or tap (mobile).
- Ambient: slow-rotating DNA helix SVG, floating leaves with depth-of-field blur, drifting blobs, occasional soft light sweeps across glass surfaces (fewer on mobile).
- Navbar: desktop glass pill morphs (width and blur) on scroll, hides on scroll down and reveals on scroll up, active indicator slides between links via shared layout animation.

F. Performance guardrails
- Animate only transform and opacity (clip-path/filter sparingly). No layout-thrashing animations.
- will-change used judiciously, IntersectionObserver pauses off-screen loops, requestAnimationFrame for canvas, devicePixelRatio capped at 2, all ambient animation paused when the tab is hidden.
- Lazy-load canvas/WebGL and GSAP plugins; code-split non-critical animation code.
- 60fps on a mid-range phone. Targets: Lighthouse mobile 90+ performance, 95+ accessibility/SEO/best practices, LCP under 2.0s on mobile, CLS near 0, INP under 200ms.
- prefers-reduced-motion: disable parallax, float loops, particles, marquee, preloader, cursor effects, and pinned scrubs; keep simple opacity fades only. Provide this as a real, tested mode.
